2. Description of Service

Undersigned is a sybil-resistant, on-chain review and trust layer that sits beside the Solana dApp Store. It does not modify, replace, or speak for the store.

  • Reviews are transactions you sign. Writing a review builds an unsigned transaction containing an SPL Memo record (a rating from 1–5, one to three fixed tags, and an optional free-text note of at most 140 characters) and a zero-value self-transfer carrying a public reference account, so the record can be found by anyone. The App never signs anything itself; your wallet, through Seed Vault / Mobile Wallet Adapter, does. The only value that ever leaves your wallet is the Solana network fee your own wallet sets — measured at approximately 0.000047 SOL per record on a Seeker device (8 September 2026). The App takes no fee for a review, issues no token, and pays nothing for reviewing.
  • Public and permanent. Once your wallet signs and the network confirms it, a review, withdrawal, or reply is public Solana blockchain data, discoverable by anyone through the App's fixed reference address, forever. A withdrawal stops the App counting and displaying a review; it does not and cannot remove the underlying text from the chain. See section 9A.
  • Who counts, and how much. A review is stored on-chain regardless of who signs it, but it is counted toward a score only if the signing wallet holds a Solana Mobile Seeker Genesis Token, read on-chain through the seeker-verify library. Staked SKR held by that wallet is read, capped, and used only to weight how much a counted review contributes to a score; the App never suggests staking, unstaking, buying, or selling SKR or any token.
  • Reading the store. The App reads the Solana dApp Store's public catalogue, star ratings, and reviews through the store's own public GraphQL feed (dappstore.solanamobile.com). This is read-only: the App never writes to the store, holds no store account, and holds no store credentials. The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Solana Mobile. Scores and reviews shown in the App are the signed opinions of individual Seeker owners, not Solana Mobile's or the dApp Store's own ratings.
  • On-device facts panel. For apps installed on your device, the App reads installed-package facts (version, signing certificate hash, and requested dangerous permissions) using Android's PackageManager, and compares them with an earlier on-device snapshot to show what changed. This is computed and stored on your device only and is never uploaded. It states facts and deltas only; it is not a claim that any app is safe, unsafe, or a scam.
  • Publisher verification. To show a publisher-verified badge on a reply, the App fetches /.well-known/undersigned.txt from the website the dApp Store lists for that app, to confirm the replying wallet is authorised by that publisher.
  • Following and alerts. You may follow apps. With your permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), the App re-reads the public ledger roughly every six hours and shows a local notification about new signed reviews on followed apps or replies to your own; nothing about you leaves your device for this check.

The App never holds, controls, or has access to your private keys or funds, and never installs, updates, or removes any app on your device; installing or opening an app is a deep link out to the Solana dApp Store, which handles it. "Solana", "Seeker", "Genesis Token" and "dApp Store" are used throughout the App descriptively, to identify the platform the App reads and writes to; they are trademarks of their respective owners and their use does not imply affiliation.
